Add More Content

Browse library.kiwix.org to find and download more ZIM files, then place them in data/zim/ and restart Kiwix.

How to Add ZIM Content
  1. Visit library.kiwix.org while online.
  2. Download the .zim file(s) you want.
  3. Place them in the data/zim/ directory of this project.
  4. Restart the Kiwix container: docker compose restart kiwix
  5. Click Open in Kiwix above to browse the content.
